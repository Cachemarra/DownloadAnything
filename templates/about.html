Download Anything

A free, open, and private tool to save video and audio from anywhere on the web — no sign-up, no tracking, no nonsense.

What it does

Paste a video link, fetch metadata, choose your preferred quality (MP4 from 144p to 4K, or 320kbps MP3 audio), and download directly to your device.

shield

Privacy first

We never store your downloads or log your activity. Files are processed in memory and deleted immediately after delivery. No cookies, no analytics.

open_source

Open source

Built on open-source technology. The downloader engine (yt-dlp) supports thousands of sites and is actively maintained by the community.

balance

Responsible use

This tool is intended for content you have the right to download — your own uploads, Creative Commons material, or public domain media. Please respect copyright.
