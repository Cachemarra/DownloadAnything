Legal & Local Usage

Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: August 2026

TL;DR — Download Anything runs 100% locally on your computer. We operate no remote servers and collect no personal data. All downloads occur directly between your device and the media source under your sole responsibility.

Standalone Desktop Software

Download Anything ("the Application", "the Software") is designed and distributed strictly as a local desktop application. We (the developers) do not host, serve, or operate any central servers, remote proxies, or media streaming services.

The Application runs entirely on your own local environment. Any media requests, link processing, and downloads are executed directly from your local hardware and internet connection.

Zero Data Collection

Because there are no central servers operated by us, we collect zero user data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect, store, or track your IP address, download history, or entered URLs.
  • We do not require accounts, sign-ups, or user credentials.
  • We do not use telemetry scripts, analytics trackers, or advertising pixels.
  • No cookies are used beyond a local browser preference setting (e.g. dark/light theme).

Local Processing & Storage

When you input a URL to fetch metadata or download media, all processing is powered locally on your machine via yt-dlp and ffmpeg:

  • Media files are downloaded directly to your local system's temporary directory during extraction.
  • Once transferred to your chosen destination folder, temporary files are immediately cleaned up locally.
  • No files or metadata ever pass through or get stored on any remote server owned by us.

Third-Party Network Connections

When executing a download, the Application sends direct network requests from your computer to the target platform hosting the media (such as YouTube, Vimeo, or other media hosts). Those third-party platforms receive requests directly from your IP address and process them under their own respective Privacy Policies and Terms of Service.

User Responsibility & Copyright Compliance

Because Download Anything operates strictly as a local software client directed by the user:

  • Sole User Responsibility: You, the individual user, are entirely responsible for the content you choose to access, stream, download, or store using this Software.
  • Legal & Term Compliance: You must ensure that your usage complies with all applicable copyright laws, local regulations, and the Terms of Service of third-party platforms.
  • Intended Usage: The Software is provided for personal archiving, fair use, public domain content, Creative Commons media, and content you have explicit rights to download.
  • Disclaimer of Liability: The developers provide this Software "as-is" without warranty of any kind, and assume no liability for misuse or copyright infringement committed by users.

Changes to This Policy

Should the Software be updated, this document will reflect the local operational model. Because we collect no personal data, your privacy remains inherently protected by design on your own local device.
