Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TL;DR — We collect zero personal data. Your downloads stay on your device. We don't track you.

Overview

Download Anything ("the App", "we", "us") is a free, open tool for downloading publicly available video and audio content. Privacy is a core value for us, not an afterthought.

This policy describes what little data is involved in running the service and how we handle it.

What we collect

We collect nothing personally identifiable. Specifically:

  • We do not use cookies (other than your locally-stored theme preference).
  • We do not create accounts or require any login.
  • We do not log IP addresses beyond what is standard in a web server access log (and these are not retained or shared).
  • We do not use analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or fingerprinting scripts.

Your downloads

When you submit a URL, the app uses yt-dlp to fetch publicly available media from the source platform on your behalf. The downloaded file is temporarily stored on the server in memory, delivered to your browser, and then immediately deleted. We never retain copies of downloaded files.

Third-party services

The App loads the following third-party resources:

  • Google Fonts – font files served from Google's CDN. Google may log your IP per their own privacy policy.
  • yt-dlp – an open-source library that communicates with the source video platform (e.g. YouTube) to retrieve media. Their terms of service and privacy policies apply to those requests.

We have no affiliation with any video platform and are not responsible for their content or policies.

Local storage

The App uses localStorage in your browser to remember your dark/light mode preference. This data never leaves your device.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. As we collect no personal data, changes are unlikely to affect your privacy in practice.
